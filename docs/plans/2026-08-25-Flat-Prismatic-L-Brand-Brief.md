# Flat Prismatic L Brand Brief

## Purpose

Replace the owner-rejected globe-L identity with a clear, proprietary app mark for a local-first creative collection workstation. The mark must function as an executable icon, splash/login symbol, and compact workstation identifier.

## Approved Direction

The identity is a **Flat Prismatic L**: exactly three interlocking geometric collection facets form one capital L silhouette. A deep-violet vertical facet carries the primary mass; a cobalt horizontal facet supplies the base; a narrow lavender facet joins them. The L must be structural, rather than a letter placed on top of another symbol.

## Constraints

| Include | Exclude |
|---|---|
| Simple icon-scale silhouette, violet/cobalt/lavender facets, transparent alpha, restrained semi-flat tonal separation | Globe, earth lines, orbit rings, hexagons, coins, wallets, NFT text, 3D extrusion, glossy glass, shard clutter, texture noise, green-screen artifacts, pasted-on lettering |

The asset is not approved for product integration until the owner accepts the rendered mark. After approval, it will replace the master PNG and ICO assets and the resulting installed payload must be reverified.

## Generation Review — 2026-08-25

The first glossy 3D concept was rejected by the owner. Two flat alternatives were then generated. Both made a clearer L silhouette, but neither is suitable for review or integration: the first retained a dark/green contaminated background and the second retained black/green chroma artifacts. Neither file will be used. The next attempt must explicitly deliver a true-alpha clean symbol with no background remnants and avoid 3D/glass treatment.
